# Code of conduct

We want participation in this project to be constructive, respectful, and safe.

Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- discuss ideas and evidence rather than attack people;
- give and receive specific, professional feedback;
- respect privacy and never post customer, profile, or credential data;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or sexualized conduct;
- disclose conflicts of interest when recommending vendors or benchmarks;
- prioritize user safety when audit convenience and account protection conflict.

Unacceptable behavior may be removed, and repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

To report conduct concerns privately, contact the maintainer through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not include Klaviyo credentials or personal data.
